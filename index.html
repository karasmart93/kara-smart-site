Optum · Instructional Designer · Feb–Sep 2024

From request to delivery: a shared intake process

The team was handling ad-hoc requests with no consistent intake. I led a shared intake-through-delivery redesign with automated Smartsheet intake, a reusable charter template, and an agile cadence. Out-of-scope work dropped 86% and the new process reached 99% adoption, measured by the team’s daily stand-up response rate.
